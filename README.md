<# 💫 About Me:
I’m Tarun Menon, a Mathematics and Actuarial Science graduate from the University of Leicester. My work sits at the intersection of statistical modelling, insurance analytics, and applied machine learning. I’m particularly interested in how traditional actuarial methods can be pushed further using AI, not in theory, but in ways that actually improve pricing, reserving, and risk assessment in practice.<br>Right now, I’m building projects around actuarial-style pricing and simulation, including AI-driven approaches to motor insurance using realistic, data-backed models. I’m looking to deepen my understanding of how these models can move beyond academic settings into production-level systems, especially where machine learning and actuarial thinking overlap. Alongside this, I’m continuing to develop my skills in predictive modelling, stochastic simulations, and data analysis using tools like R, Python, SQL, and Power BI.<br>If you explore my work, you’ll find projects ranging from Monte Carlo simulations and derivatives pricing to analytics in sport like cricket and Formula 1. I tend to approach problems the same way regardless of domain: break them down, model them properly, and focus on what actually drives outcomes. If it involves data and uncertainty, I’m interested.<br>I’m currently aiming for actuarial roles within general insurance, particularly in pricing and risk modelling, where I can apply both statistical rigour and practical thinking.
